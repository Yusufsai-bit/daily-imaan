Privacy Policy

Effective: May 2, 2026
Daily Imaan uses no advertising, no third-party analytics, and no behavioural tracking. We do not ask you to create an account, share an email, or sign in.

1. Information stored on your device

The app stores the following data locally using standard mobile storage (AsyncStorage):

  • Your bookmarks (saved ayat and hadith).
  • Your reading progress and consecutive-day streak (including streak-freeze grace days).
  • Your daily intentions / good-deed checkmarks (kept for 365 days, then pruned).
  • Your settings (dark mode, reciter, prayer calculation method, juristic school, reminder times, dhikr target, Arabic font size, continuous-play preference).
  • Cached tafsir text (Ibn Kathir, Abridged) for offline reading.
  • Cached prayer times for your area, including rounded coordinates used for the calculation.
  • The most recent surah you opened.

2. Optional cloud sync (anonymous)

To prevent loss of your streak and bookmarks on reinstall, Daily Imaan can optionally mirror your data to Supabase. When active:

  • The app creates an anonymous session on first launch — no email, name, or login required. Your identity is a randomly-generated UUID stored on-device.
  • Data is uploaded to a private row keyed to that UUID, protected by Row Level Security so only your session can read or write it.
  • We never see any identifier that could link the row to you as a person.
  • You can disable cloud sync at any time from Settings.
  • Supabase privacy: supabase.com/privacy

3. Location

If you grant location permission, the app uses your coordinates only to calculate prayer times and point the Qibla compass. Coordinates are sent to AlAdhan (api.aladhan.com) and never stored beyond the local cache. You can revoke access at any time from system settings.

4. Notifications

Daily reminders and prayer-time alerts are scheduled locally on your device. We do not transmit a per-user notification payload and cannot see whether or when a notification fires.

5. Third-party services

  • AlAdhan — prayer times and Hijri dates. aladhan.com/privacy-policy
  • Quran.com Foundation — verse text and tafsir. quran.foundation/privacy
  • Alquran.cloud — audio recitation. alquran.cloud/privacy-policy
  • Supabase (cloud sync only) — anonymous UUID and state document. supabase.com/privacy
  • Sentry (crashes only) — anonymised stack trace, app version, device model. sentry.io/privacy

6. Crash reporting

If the app crashes, anonymized diagnostic information (error message, stack trace, OS/app version, device model) may be sent to Sentry. No bookmarks, location, cached text, or personal data are included. Disable from Settings → Privacy → Crash reports.

7. Children's privacy

Daily Imaan is suitable for all ages. There is no chat, no user-generated content, no advertising, and no in-app purchases.

8. Your rights

  • Erase all local data: uninstall the app or clear app data from system settings.
  • Erase your cloud row: email us with the date of uninstall and we will purge the matching row.
  • Export your data: email us and we will send the contents of your row.
  • Disable cloud sync: Settings → Privacy → Cloud sync → off.

Rights under GDPR (EU/UK), CCPA (California), Australian Privacy Principles, and any other applicable law are honoured.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the "Effective" date above and, where the change is material, surface a notice in the app on next launch.

10. Contact

Questions or deletion requests: support@dailyimaan.com

We are based in Australia. The current operator is the developer named in the App Store listing.
