# Daily Imaan — First 20 Instagram Posts

  Each post is **1080×1350** (Instagram portrait, 4:5 ratio — the largest aspect ratio that won't be cropped in-feed).

  **Design language**
  - Sage green (#1A6B4A) primary + gold (#C8933C) hairline accent
  - Background: cream → pale sage gradient with a soft sage halo behind the verse
  - Arabic typeset in **Amiri** (open-source naskh, Open Font License) with full Uthmani diacritics
  - English typeset in **DejaVu Serif** as a stand-in for the brand body face
  - Every post cites the source verbatim — Saheeh International for the translation, surah:ayah for the citation
  - No emoji inside the sacred text. No hashtag stacks > 3 in any caption

  **Posting cadence**
  - Recommended: post 02–03 a week (8–12 weeks of cover from this batch)
  - Best times for Islamic-content audiences: 7–9pm local, especially Thursday/Friday evenings (Friday Jumu'ah engagement spike)

  **Carousel option**
  The "/ 20" mark in the corner means these can also be posted as 5×4 carousels (5 posts per carousel × 4 carousels) if you prefer fewer feed entries. Order them by theme:
  - 1, 5, 8, 11, 18 — comfort and presence
  - 2, 6, 13, 15, 20 — patience and reliance
  - 3, 14, 16, 19 — the power and mercy of Allah
  - 4, 9, 10, 17 — worship and asking

  **Hashtag bank** (mix-and-match, max 3 per post per Instagram best practice for niche accounts):
  `#dailyimaan #quran #saheeh #islamicapp #dhikr #salah #imaan #tawakkul #sabr #dua #fatihah #ilm #quranquotes #verbatim`
